## 2. GAME IN ONE PARAGRAPH

A mobile ACTIVE farming-tycoon, not an idle game. Loop: buy seeds, plant, water, harvest, carry produce, sell to customers and markets, upgrade, expand. Crops grow FAST (seconds to minutes) so the player is always busy and always about to be rewarded, but every step is the player's decision and touch. Story: the player inherits a mortgaged, ruined farm and a 25,000 debt and climbs from total bankruptcy to super-wealth across 7 acts (about 30 hours) plus an endless tail. Landscape phone game, one-finger-first.

## 3. ENGINE AND ARCHITECTURE (final decisions)

- Engine: Godot 4.x latest stable, GDScript with static typing, 2D (Godot's 2D canvas renderer). Reason: text-based scenes an agent can generate, built-in Skeleton2D/Bone2D/Polygon2D/Line2D/AnimationPlayer for real rigging, CanvasItem shaders, Light2D with normal maps, headless tooling, Android export when ordered.
- Renderer: choose Mobile or Compatibility by the P0 benchmark.
- Skeletons: Skeleton2D + Bone2D + weighted Polygon2D meshes + AnimationPlayer, plus a procedural animation layer (breathing, spring bones, look-at, tail/ear follow-through).
- Data driven: crops, recipes, buildings, customers, contracts, acts, dialogue in `data/` as JSON or Resource files. Adding a crop or recipe is one entry.
- Numbers: big-number type (mantissa + exponent) with K/M/B/T formatting. Net worth reaches 1T+.
- Save: versioned JSON in `user://`, atomic write (temp file then rename), autosave every 30 s and on pause/focus-out, backup copy, export/import save string.
- Localization: Persian (RTL, correct shaping through Godot TextServer) and English. Font: Vazirmatn (OFL) for Persian plus a matching Latin display font. Persian numerals option.
- Layout:
  - `amberfield/project.godot`
  - `amberfield/game/` (scenes, scripts by domain: farm, world, economy, npc, story, ui, audio, fx, save)
  - `amberfield/data/`
  - `amberfield/assets/` (generated and processed only: atlases, rigs, audio, fonts)
  - `amberfield/tools/` (Python and GDScript helpers: artforge, partforge, styleforge, capture, freeze, simbot, audioforge)
  - `/tmp/amberfield_dl/` (downloaded tools and fonts)

## 4. NEW ART STYLE: "LUMEN GOUACHE"

Goal: extremely eye-pleasing, one unified look, generated entirely by code so that every asset shares the same hand-painted feel.

- Painted surfaces: soft color masses, dry-brush edges, paper grain, colored ink outline (a darker shade of the fill, never pure black).
- Luminous light: warm rim light on every object, cool tinted shadows, leaf translucency, glowing windows and lamps, god rays, drifting cloud shadows.
- Hue-shifted palette ramps (4-5 tones) per zone and per time of day, applied through grading LUTs. Shadows lean blue/violet, highlights lean warm gold.
- Depth: 5-layer parallax, tilt-shift blur at screen edges, atmospheric perspective, fog planes.
- Motion everywhere: one shared wind field drives grass, crops, trees, cloth and smoke. Springy UI. Squash-and-stretch on every interaction.
- Offline finishing tools (Python, in `tools/styleforge/`), applied to everything the generators output: palette_remap, painterly_filter, normal_from_sprite, ao_bake, rim_mask, edge_ink, halo_fix, atlas_pack (padding, ETC2/ASTC-friendly sizes), contact_sheet.
- Runtime shaders (each with HIGH/MEDIUM/LOW variants): painterly_post, sprite_lit, wind_sway, leaf_translucency, water, growth_pop, grade_lut, godrays, tilt_shift, paper_grain, vignette.
- Quality tiers auto-selected from measured frame time. Optional 30 fps battery saver.

## 5. ART CREATION, RIGGING AND THE LOOP

There is no asset downloading. You build a small family of generators in `tools/artforge/` and then use them to produce every asset. Each generator is parametric (seeded random plus parameters), so one generator yields many varied assets.

ArtForge core library (shared by all generators): signed distance field shapes and boolean blends, bezier and catmull-rom curves, brush-stroke simulation (variable width, dry-brush edge, bristle noise), simplex/fbm/Worley noise, color ramp and hue-shift math, mesh triangulation with interior vertices, ambient occlusion and rim-light baking, seamless tiling, seeded variation.

Generator families:
- plantgen: crops, bushes, flowers, grass blades (petal/leaf/stem curves, L-systems, 5 growth stages, ripe shimmer).
- treegen: trunks, recursive branching, leaf-cluster blobs, fruit, seasonal palettes.
- charactergen: parametric body parts (head, torso, arms, legs, hands, hair, clothes, accessories) with body-shape, skin, outfit and palette parameters; portrait faces with blink, mouth shapes and 5 emotions.
- animalgen: quadruped, bird and insect templates with parameterized proportions, fur or feather patterns, tails, ears, wings.
- buildinggen: modular kit (walls, roofs, doors, windows, chimneys, planks, stone, tiles, signs) with ruined, restored and grand states and animated parts.
- machinegen: vehicles, tools and machines from primitives with rotating and bobbing parts.
- terraingen: soil, grass, path, cobble, water, cliff and fence tiles from noise and stroke simulation, seamless.
- propgen: rocks, crates, barrels, sacks, lamps, signs, bridges.
- fxgen: particle textures, coin and sparkle bursts, splashes, dust, weather, god rays, fog.
- skygen: sky gradients, painted clouds, cloud shadows, stars.
- uigen: frames, buttons, icons and animated states in the same painted style.
- audioforge (Python numpy synth, ffmpeg to OGG): foley, ambience, music, voice blips (see P10.4).

PartForge (`tools/partforge/`), in order:
- Take parametric parts from the generators, not flat images, so no segmentation or inpainting is needed.
- Build a triangulated mesh per part with interior vertices.
- Auto-weight vertices to bones by distance/heat.
- Emit `Skeleton2D` scene + `AnimationPlayer` libraries.
- Generate clips procedurally from a shared clip library: idle_breathe, walk, run, plant, water, harvest, carry, hit_tool, cheer, sad, talk, eat, sleep.
- Render a preview contact sheet of every clip.
- Non-skeletal entities (plants, trees, cloth, flags, smoke) are animated by shaders and the shared wind field.

Per-asset LOOP (repeat at least 3 times, at most 8):
1. Generate by code. 2. StyleForge to Lumen Gouache. 3. Rig or shader-animate. 4. Author clips. 5. Place in a test scene under 3 lightings (day, dusk, night) on 3 backgrounds. 6. Capture frames with the harness. 7. Score. 8. Fix the weakest score in the generator (not by hand-patching one output) and repeat.

Rubric (1-10 each): Style Match, Phone Readability (clear silhouette at 64 px), Motion Quality, Technical Cleanliness (no halos, jaggies, seams; atlas packed; cost within budget), Life (variation, secondary motion), Charm. An asset passes only when every score is at least 8 and the average is at least 8.5, and the freeze test passes.

## 6. STORY BIBLE

Setting: valley of Lowmere, a fictional country. Farm: Amberfield. Tone: warm, funny, dramatic, hopeful, never preachy.

Premise: the player (name chosen, two visual variants) returns to Grandpa Odell's farm after his death. It is mortgaged for 25,000 and will be auctioned in 60 in-game days. The player has 12 coins, a rusty hoe, a leaking can, a tin of 5 turnip seeds and a stray dog, Pip.

Twist: Verdane Agro has been bankrupting valley farms with engineered debt loans and a "miracle seed" that fails on purpose, so it can buy the land cheap. Grandpa's Amber Ledger has the proof. The player wins by out-growing Verdane, then buying it out.

Cast (each needs a voice sheet: speech rhythm, verbal habits, secrets, how they talk when scared or happy):
- Player: stubborn, self-deprecating, learns fast.
- Pip: dog companion, buffs nearby crops.
- Grandpa Odell: seen through letters, ledger margin notes and memory scenes.
- Mira: village shopkeeper, sharp tongue, soft heart, first ally.
- Mr. Halden Crowe: banker, cold at first, becomes an ally after seeing the player's integrity.
- Lucian Verdane: charming rival heir of Verdane Agro. Ending path decides ally or ruined.
- Beeka: old seed seller, gives rare seeds and riddles.
- Dr. Sana: botanist, finds the sabotage evidence, unlocks crop breeding of hybrids.
- Tariq: mechanic, builds machines and vehicles.
- Auntie Zahra: baker, unlocks processing and recipes.

Acts (each opens with a painted act curtain and closes with a net-worth ledger tally):
- Act 0 Prologue: auction hill and the letter. Net worth -25,000.
- Act 1 Rock Bottom (0-1.5 h): 60-day foreclosure clock, first crops, first sales, debt cleared. Ends at 0. If the clock runs out there is no game over: a scripted Second Notice scene adds a fee and interest and the story continues.
- Act 2 First Green (1.5-4 h): market stall, first helper, land expansion, Lucian arrives. Ends near 40K.
- Act 3 The Market Rises (4-8 h): contracts, processing (mill, kitchen, dairy, jam, pickles), animals, sabotage. Ends near 600K.
- Act 4 The Cooperative (8-13 h): riverside irrigation, orchard hills, bees, trees, village co-op, Sana's evidence. Ends near 12M.
- Act 5 The Estate (13-19 h): highland greenhouses, tractors, trucks, staff, climate control. Ends near 400M.
- Act 6 The Empire (19-26 h): port, export, corporation, drones, boardroom confrontation, the ledger's secret page. Ends near 30B.
- Act 7 Legacy (26-32 h): Verdane buyout, choice between Tycoon and Steward paths (both end super-rich, different epilogue and cosmetics), Grandpa's memory bench, final painting. Then Endless Tiers.

Dialogue rules:
- Write real, spoken dialogue: contractions, interruptions, subtext, running jokes, callbacks, humor under pressure. No exposition dumps, no "as you know".
- Persian must be colloquial (محاوره‌ای) and natural, not translated-sounding. English equal quality. Both written natively, not machine-translated.
- Bubble limit about 140 characters. Tap to advance. Voice blips instead of voiceover.
- Minimum: 90 lines per act, 700 story lines total, 300 NPC ambient lines, all in both languages.
- Portraits are rigged: blink, mouth flap, 5 emotions, subtle breathing.

Painted scenes ("curtains"): each is a multi-plane composition (5-8 depth layers) built in-engine from generated assets and rendered through the Lumen Gouache painterly filter, with rigged characters, parallax camera moves, animated wind, smoke, birds and light. Sky, hills, clouds and distant layers come from skygen and terraingen. Act transitions use a painted theater-curtain wipe.

## 7. ECONOMY AND GROWTH-SPEED SPEC

- Start: 12 coins, debt 25,000. HUD shows a debt/ledger bar and 3 next-goal icons with fill bars.
- Crop tiers (base growth time, sell margin 80-200 percent per cycle before modifiers):
  - T1 15-45 s: radish, lettuce, turnip.
  - T2 1-2.5 min: carrot, potato, wheat, onion, cabbage.
  - T3 3-6 min: tomato, corn, cucumber, strawberry (regrows), eggplant, pepper, sunflower.
  - T4 6-12 min: pumpkin, watermelon, blueberry (regrows), grape vine, saffron.
  - T5 perennial trees, fruit cycle 3-8 min: apple, pomegranate, olive, pistachio, date.
  - T6 luxury and hybrid crops, 10-20 min, high value.
- Early money doubling time about 45-90 s, slowing smoothly. There is always something ripening within 15 s and an affordable upgrade within 2 minutes.
- Speed sources: tool upgrades, fertilizer, sprinklers, grow lamps, greenhouse, helpers, weather. No premium-currency timers, no energy, no daily caps.
- Selling: market stall with rigged customers who have orders and patience bars; prices move by supply/demand random walk plus events; quality stars multiply value; optional tap-timing haggle for +10-25 percent; wholesale truck for instant lower-price bulk; contracts board with reputation tiers.
- Not idle: no offline income. While the app is closed, crops keep growing and wait ripe for up to 12 hours, then slowly wilt down to 50 percent value, never destroyed. Helpers work only while the game is running.
- Time: 1 game day = 90 s, 1 season = 12 days, 1 year = 4 seasons. Tunable.
- Cost growth 1.07-1.15 per level, milestone levels (25/50/100) give unique perks.
- Game-time targets: first sale within 60 s of control, first upgrade within 2 min, first debt installment within 20 min.
- New mechanic or unlock every 10-15 minutes during the first 5 hours, then every 30-60 minutes.
